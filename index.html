Questions

Before your session

How do I book a tattoo?

Send us your idea, placement, approximate size and a few reference images. We'll reply with availability and the next steps.

Do you take walk-ins?

We are primarily appointment-based so each artist has enough time for custom work. Limited walk-in availability may happen on selected days.

How much does a tattoo cost?

Pricing depends on size, placement, style and artist. After reviewing your idea, we'll give you a clear estimate before the appointment.
